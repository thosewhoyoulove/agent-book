# Audio Credits / 音频素材致谢

These files are **original, freely licensed** tracks and sound effects.  
They are **not** official or ripped audio from Stardew Valley, Celeste, or Hollow Knight.

本目录中的文件均为**原创、可自由使用**的音乐与音效，  
**不是**《星露谷物语》《蔚蓝》《空洞骑士》的官方原声或提取资源。

All licenses below were verified on the source pages (OpenGameArt.org and/or Kenney.nl).  
Every item is **CC0 1.0** (public domain dedication). Attribution is not required, but it is appreciated.

以下许可均已在原始页面核对，全部为 **CC0 1.0**（公共领域）。署名并非强制，但建议保留本说明。

Technical notes / 技术说明：

- BGM converted to Ogg Vorbis, 44.1 kHz stereo, about 128–160 kbps.
- SFX exported as 16-bit PCM WAV, 44.1 kHz stereo.
- Some SFX were trimmed to about 1 second with a short fade-out.
- `hollow-surprise.wav` was pitched down by 5 semitones for a darker sting.
- Peak levels were normalized to about −1 dB to avoid clipping.

BGM 已转为 Ogg Vorbis（44.1 kHz 立体声，约 128–160 kbps）。  
SFX 已导出为 16-bit PCM WAV（44.1 kHz 立体声）。部分音效裁切至约 1 秒并做淡出。  
`hollow-surprise.wav` 降低了 5 个半音，使其更偏深沉庄严。峰值约归一化到 −1 dB，避免削波。
